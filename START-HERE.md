## 1. Read the Whole Book

Start with the [Preface](PREFACE.md), then continue to [Chapter 0 — The Globant Question](chapters/part-1-understanding-globant/00-the-globant-question.md). The chapter navigation will carry you through all five Parts.

## 2. Understand the Sales-Engineering Question

Begin with [Part III — How Deals Happen](chapters/part-3-how-deals-happen/README.md), especially the [Technical–Commercial Bridge](chapters/part-3-how-deals-happen/30-the-technical-commercial-bridge.md). Then use Part V's [Independent Sales Engineering Model](chapters/part-5-lessons-for-small-firms/62-the-independent-sales-engineering-model.md) to test how that function might work at smaller scale.

## 3. Study Real Customer and Partner Examples

Read [Part II — Follow the Customers](chapters/part-2-customers/README.md) and [Part IV — The Partner Ecosystem](chapters/part-4-partner-ecosystem/README.md). The [Customer Index](appendix/customer-index.md) and [Partner Index](appendix/partner-index.md) provide evidence-led shortcuts.
