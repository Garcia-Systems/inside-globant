# Research Notes and Open Questions

These notes are deliberately not polished conclusions. They preserve the boundary between evidence and an attractive story.

## Evidence baseline

* FY2024 Form 20-F is the coherent audited quantitative baseline used in Part I. Future work should compare the latest filed annual report line by line rather than patching isolated quarterly numbers into FY2024.
* Globant pages are useful for taxonomy and leads. For outcomes, seek customer-side or jointly issued material and dated archived versions.
* Office locations cannot be converted into a delivery-center map. Customer relationships cannot be converted into revenue estimates.

## How Globant wins customers

**Known:** acquisitions, partnerships, sponsorships, public thought leadership, a client portfolio, and account scale are observable routes to visibility or capability. **Unknown:** origination source, qualification standards, sales cycle, competitive set, procurement route, and executive sponsor for most named engagements.

Research leads: founder interviews with precise dates; customer procurement disclosures; conference panels featuring both parties; acquisition filings identifying client transfer; partnership marketplaces; public-sector awards where applicable.

## Sales and solution organization

Public role titles may confirm that sales, account, consulting, architecture, Studio, and delivery jobs exist at a moment. They do not establish a universal reporting line. Capture dated vacancies only if the original page remains available. Test whether Globant uses “solution architect,” “business hacker,” “client partner,” “account director,” or other terms, and distinguish title from function.

Questions:

1. Who owns qualification and forecast?
2. When do Studio specialists enter discovery?
3. Who validates staff availability, architecture, estimate, price, and risk?
4. Is there a formal transition from presales to delivery, and who retains continuity?
5. How do acquired consultancies connect with broader delivery?

## Scoping and commercials

The Form 20-F describes contract and revenue-recognition categories at portfolio level. It does not identify pricing for customer stories. Future research must not label work fixed-price, T&M, managed service, or outcome-based without a source.

Look for statements of work in public procurement repositories, litigation exhibits, customer investor presentations, or joint executive interviews. Record whether discovery is separately contracted and how change control is described.

## Account expansion

Large-customer metrics make expansion economically plausible, but do not reveal mechanism. For a dated customer evidence chain, map first public work, subsequent capability/region, named leaders, and language suggesting renewal. Do not equate a later press release with continuous work unless stated.

## US customer interaction with distributed teams

The aggregate model supports geographic distribution. Account-level location is unknown. Seek customer engineering blogs, conference talks, credible employee presentations, and joint case studies that name team cities, collaboration cadence, or governance. Treat anonymous employment reviews as leads, not facts.

## Strong Part II candidates

* **FIFA:** unusually strong official counterparty announcement; distinguish technology-provider scope from sponsorship and later events.
* **LATAM Airlines:** directly relevant to Latin American origin plus multinational customer; needs airline-side sources.
* **Nissan:** useful experience/automotive example; seek regional entity, dates, and customer corroboration.
* **Santander:** valuable regulated-industry case; determine which Santander entity and geography.
* **Google:** strategically important early-history claim but possibly thin public detail; keep unknowns explicit.

## Contradictions and ambiguities to monitor

* “Employee,” “Glober,” and “technology, design and innovation professional” are not interchangeable. Preserve filing definitions and dates.
* “North America revenue” is not “US revenue.” Do not subtract or infer without disclosed country data.
* Studio lists and category names change. Date snapshots and avoid treating present taxonomy as historical.
* Acquisition announcements state intentions; later filings are needed to assess integration.
* Customer-reported outcome, provider-reported outcome, and independently measured outcome carry different evidentiary weight.
