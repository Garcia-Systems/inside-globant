# Glossary

Definitions are used in this book; firms may use the same words differently.

### Client partner

A customer-facing leader who coordinates relationship context, account priorities, and the provider's response. The exact authority and title vary by organization.

**Why it matters in this book:** A client partner may connect commercial continuity with solution and delivery teams, but the title alone does not prove responsibility on a particular Globant account.

### Staff augmentation

A services arrangement centered on adding external people or capacity to a customer-directed team, rather than transferring responsibility for a defined project or managed service.

**Why it matters in this book:** It is one possible purchase form, not a sufficient description of everything Globant sells. See also [outsourcing](#outsourcing) and [time and materials](#time-and-materials-tm).

### Account expansion

Additional work won with an existing customer, whether in the same program, capability, business unit, or geography.

**Why it matters in this book:** The term helps distinguish the capabilities, roles, or commercial commitments involved in a technology-services engagement.


### Account management

The function of sustaining customer context, relationships, commercial health, and coordinated response across engagements. It is not assumed to be a specific Globant title.

**Why it matters in this book:** The term helps distinguish the capabilities, roles, or commercial commitments involved in a technology-services engagement.


### Billable work

Effort chargeable under a client agreement. A firm's accounting definition may differ from a manager's utilization definition.

**Why it matters in this book:** The term helps distinguish the capabilities, roles, or commercial commitments involved in a technology-services engagement.
