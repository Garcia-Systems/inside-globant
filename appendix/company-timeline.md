# Company Timeline

This selective timeline records milestones relevant to the business model, not every award or acquisition. Acquisition rationales are the company's stated rationale, not independently verified integration outcomes.

| Year | Event | Why it matters | Source |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 2003 | Martín Migoya, Guibert Englebienne, Martín Umaran, and Néstor Nocetti found Globant in Buenos Aires. | Establishes the Latin American origin of a globally oriented engineering company. | [Globant — About](https://www.globant.com/about) |
| 2006 | Google becomes a publicly cited early major customer; Globant expands its international trajectory. | Illustrates the credibility problem inherent in selling from a young regional firm to global technology buyers; public summaries do not reveal the sales process. | [Globant — About](https://www.globant.com/about) |
| 2014 | Globant completes its NYSE initial public offering under ticker GLOB. | Adds public capital-market access, disclosure, governance, and acquisition currency. | [SEC filings](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=1557860&owner=exclude) |
| 2016 | Globant announces acquisition of US strategy and design firm WAE. | Intended to add upstream strategy/design and strengthen US presence. | [Globant announcement](https://www.globant.com/news/globant-acquires-leading-digital-strategy-and-design-firm) |
| 2020 | Globant announces acquisition of Spain-based Bluecap. | Intended to deepen strategic consulting, especially financial services, and European reach. | [Globant announcement](https://www.globant.com/news/globant-acquires-bluecap) |
| 2021 | Globant announces acquisition of UK-based CloudShift. | Adds Salesforce specialization and UK market capability. | [Globant announcement](https://www.globant.com/news/globant-acquires-cloudshift) |
| 2022 | Globant announces acquisition of GeneXus. | Adds a low-code platform and ecosystem, illustrating expansion beyond labor capacity alone. | [Globant announcement](https://www.globant.com/news/globant-acquires-genexus) |
| 2022 | FIFA and Globant announce a multiyear partnership. | Public example combining platform work, a major global institution, and sponsorship. | [FIFA announcement](https://www.fifa.com/about-fifa/commercial/media-releases/fifa-and-globant-announce-multi-year-partnership) |
| 2024 | Globant reports $2.421 billion FY2024 revenue and 31,107 Globers at year-end. | Audited scale baseline for the services engine. | [FY2024 Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=1557860&owner=exclude) |

Dates and metrics should be revalidated against the cited filings when a later edition changes the financial baseline. The [key company metrics](key-company-metrics.md) keeps period definitions separate from this strategic chronology.
