# Opportunity Qualification Framework

A company is not an opportunity. Record evidence for each element; “unknown” is acceptable, provided the next action tests it.

| Element | Core questions | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| problem | what fails or cannot happen; for whom? | observation, data, stakeholder account |
| impact | cost, revenue, control, customer or strategy consequence? | baseline and method |
| urgency | why decide now; what happens if not? | external event or compounding exposure |
| stakeholders | outcome owner, operators, technical/security/procurement actors? | access and roles |
| technical fit | systems, data, constraints, scale, required specialty? | artifacts, spike, specialist review |
| commercial fit | budget path, alternatives, contract form, range? | buyer discussion, not provider hope |
| mutual fit | information access, collaboration, ethics and fair risk? | observed behavior |
| next decision | what will be decided, by whom, using what evidence, when? | mutual commitment |

## Signals to investigate

Legacy exposure; integration backlog; manual exceptions; growth; acquisition; new product; migration; AI/data initiative; constrained hiring; temporary capacity; scarce specialty. A signal is not qualification.

## Exit conditions

Pause or decline when impact is immaterial, sponsor/access is absent, required capability cannot be controlled, timeline is physically implausible, procurement is knowingly impossible, free solution extraction replaces mutual discovery, or conduct threatens people, law, security or reputation.
