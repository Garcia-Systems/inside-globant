# Key Company Metrics

These figures are an educational scale reference, not a valuation dashboard. They use one audited period so that revenue, workforce, client, and geographic measures are not accidentally compared across incompatible dates. **FY2024 remains this edition's verified filing baseline; it is not presented as a current 2026 snapshot.** Readers should consult the SEC filing index for later filings.

## Reading rules

1. A year-end workforce count and full-year revenue describe different measurement windows.
2. Revenue geography does not reveal where delivery occurred.
3. Customer thresholds describe recognized revenue, not pipeline, bookings, or relationship quality.
4. The filing is authoritative for its period; later press releases may be newer but unaudited and should be dated as such.
