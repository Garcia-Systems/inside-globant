# Globant vs. a Small Global Engineering Firm

This is an analytical comparison, not a claim that either model is superior for every buyer.

| Capability | Globant evidence/model | Small-firm analogue | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| market presence | global locations and commercial expense | focused market owner, travel or partner | address alone is not access |
| brand/references | major public relationships | niche, permissioned cases | specificity outranks borrowed prestige |
| industry breadth | multiple marketed industries | one or two workflows | decline unsupported sectors |
| specialization | broad Studio portfolio | few deep practices | do not rename generalists as practices |
| discovery/consulting | marketed consulting/design services | structured workshop or paid discovery | public evidence does not reveal Globant's universal process |
| architecture/presales | distributed specialist roles | protected senior architect | validate before promise |
| delivery | large distributed workforce | bounded team/capacity | smaller firm must expose concentration risk |
| governance | enterprise program capacity | named lead, cadence, risk/decision log | lightweight does not mean absent |
| ecosystem | many documented alliances | one or two meaningful partners | badge is not outcome |
| procurement | established corporate infrastructure | reusable diligence pack and advisers | buyer requirements vary |
| account growth | large-client relationships | outcome review and next qualification | expansion is not entitlement |
