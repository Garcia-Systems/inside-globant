# 68 — The “Globant at 100 People” Model

The title is deliberately provocative. The objective is **not** to recreate Globant. Imagine a 100-person international firm, mostly engineers, with three credible specialties and good delivery history but limited U.S. commercial infrastructure. What minimum system could compete credibly for suitable work?

```text
chosen market → relationship → discovery → technical–commercial translation
→ specialists → feasible solution → controlled proposal → customer decision
→ governed delivery → outcome/account learning
```

## Minimum viable commercial system

| Function | Minimum control | Possible owner(s) |
|---|---|---|
| market | explicit ideal problem/customer and exclusions | founder + market lead/external adviser |
| access | consistent relationship development, not mass spam | salesperson, founder, partner |
| qualification | evidence, fit, buying path and next decision recorded | market lead + technical lead |
| discovery | workflow, value, stakeholders, systems and uncertainty | solution consultant, architect, founder |
| translation | business/technical loop and decision artifacts | sales engineer/architect pairing |
| solution/estimate | technical and delivery approval; assumptions visible | architect + delivery lead + engineers |
| proposal/commercial | one version of scope, price, responsibilities and terms | account owner + finance/counsel as needed |
| delivery | named accountable lead, cadence, risks and escalation | delivery lead/project lead |
| account learning | outcomes, reference permission, next problem independently qualified | account owner + delivery lead |

A plausible allocation is one full-time market/account lead, a founder sponsor, a rotating senior architect with protected presales capacity, and a delivery leader involved before signature. An external representative may supply access and facilitation; specialists join only qualified pursuits. This is a model, not a staffing prescription. Volume, deal complexity, customer maturity and founder skill determine whether roles combine.

## Global scale versus 100 people

| Dimension | Globant at global scale | 100-person engineering company |
|---|---|---|
| brand | broad public recognition | niche, evidence-led reputation |
| sales/accounts | portfolio of roles and regions | one accountable owner per chosen account |
| presales | broad consulting/specialist access | protected rotating architect and selective advisers |
| industry expertise | multiple industries | one or two workflows deeply understood |
| specialists | many Studios/capabilities | three real specialties; decline the rest |
| delivery | large distributed capacity | bounded mobilization and senior visibility |
| partnerships | multiple strategic alliances | one or two used platforms |
| procurement | mature corporate infrastructure | reusable diligence pack and advisers |
| marketing | broad brand/content/events | concentrated insight and cases |
| customer success | account and delivery machinery | joint outcome review and named follow-up |
| geography | office/delivery network | designed overlap, travel, local help when justified |

Three rules hold it together: no commercial promise without technical validation; no technical estimate without customer assumptions; no handoff without decision context. Track qualified pipeline, presales hours, win/loss reasons, estimate variance, gross margin, delivery health, referenceability and expansion from demonstrated outcomes. Metrics diagnose; they do not replace judgment.
