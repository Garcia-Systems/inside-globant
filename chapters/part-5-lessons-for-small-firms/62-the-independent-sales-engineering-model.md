# Chapter 62 — The Independent Sales Engineering Model

Could an independent technical/commercial professional connect an overseas engineering firm with U.S. demand? Yes, structurally—but the label hides materially different arrangements, and public evidence does not establish one universal best model.

## Possible structures

| Structure | Primary contribution | Typical economic basis | Central risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| referral partner | qualified introduction | contingent fee | low context; incentive to maximize introductions |
| independent sales representative | develop and advance opportunities | commission | authority and promise control |
| business-development consultant | market/account work | retainer or project fee | activity without outcomes |
| fractional sales leader | system, coaching, pipeline governance | retainer plus variable | part-time capacity |
| presales/solution consultant | discovery, qualification, solution support | day rate, retainer, success component | disconnect from delivery |
| channel/consulting partner | complementary offer or route | referral, resale, margin or joint work | customer ownership and conflicts |
| prime/subcontract model | one party contracts; another delivers | subcontract price/margin | transparency, liability and quality |
| fractional technical leader | architecture and credibility | retainer/day rate | insufficient commercial ownership |

Technology ecosystems formally recognize varied partner routes—see, for example, [AWS Partner Network](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/) and [Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program](https://partner.microsoft.com/). Those product ecosystems demonstrate plural structures, not that their terms apply to independent engineering representation.

```text
GLOBAL ENGINEERING COMPANY
          ↕
U.S.-FACING TECHNICAL / COMMERCIAL PARTNER
          ↕
U.S. CUSTOMER
```

The middle party creates limited value by forwarding names. Higher value requires some combination of choosing a market, earning relationships, diagnosing problems, testing technical fit, orchestrating specialists, shaping a buyable offer, preserving decision context into delivery, and developing the account after results. Each additional function requires competence, time, authority, information access and accountability.

## Controls before opportunity flow

The parties should define territories/accounts, exclusivity, conflicts, use of marks, authority to quote or bind, qualification standards, information security, customer ownership, presales cost, expense approval, compensation triggers, refund/termination treatment, delivery handoff, tail periods and dispute handling. These are negotiation topics, not legal advice; qualified counsel should address applicable law, tax, agency, licensing, employment and anti-corruption questions.

The representative must never sell unseen capacity. Engineers must never assume the representative has secured budget or authority. A joint opportunity review should record customer evidence, technical unknowns, capacity, next decision, and who may promise what.

The model works only if it supplies missing functions more economically and credibly than hiring, founder-led sales, a vendor partnership, or customer-led specification. [Chapter 63](63-how-the-economics-might-work.md) makes those tradeoffs visible.

## Questions to Think About

1. Which assumptions in the the independent sales engineering model model would need evidence before a smaller firm acted on it?
2. Which capability should remain accountable inside the engineering firm, even if some work is external?
3. What customer evidence would show that this approach is reducing risk rather than merely adding activity?
