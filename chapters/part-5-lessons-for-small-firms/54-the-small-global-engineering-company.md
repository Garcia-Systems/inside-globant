# Chapter 54 — The Small Global Engineering Company

There is no single “offshore company.” The [World Trade Organization's work on digitally delivered services](https://www.wto.org/english/res_e/booksp_e/dtd2023_e.pdf) treats cross-border digital delivery as a large and growing mode of trade, while the [World Bank's Digital Progress and Trends Report](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/digital-progress-and-trends-report) emphasizes uneven participation and skills. These sources justify a global category, not a stereotype about any country or team.

## Overlapping business forms

* **Development agencies** sell projects, often spanning design and build.
* **Staff-augmentation firms** supply individuals or teams under customer direction.
* **Managed development teams** accept more responsibility for team operation and outputs.
* **Product-development companies** help shape and build another company's product; some also own products.
* **Cloud, data, AI, security, or platform consultancies** lead with a specialty and may implement it.
* **Engineering boutiques** compete through scarce depth, domain knowledge, or senior attention.

A firm can occupy several categories in one year—or one engagement. “Agency,” “consultancy,” “nearshore,” and “staff augmentation” describe different dimensions: proposition, responsibility, geography, and commercial form. Buyers should ask who defines the problem, directs work, owns outcomes, and bears estimation risk rather than infer those answers from a label.

## Regions are operating contexts, not quality scores

Latin America may offer substantial overlap with North American working hours. India and Southeast Asia can support follow-the-sun or deliberately staggered collaboration. Central and Eastern European locations may overlap partly with both Western Europe and North America. Those are longitude observations, not claims about talent or communication. Daylight-saving rules, individual schedules, customer location, and team design matter more than a regional label.

Likewise, averages conceal firms. The [EF English Proficiency Index](https://www.ef.com/wwen/epi/) is based on a self-selected test-taking population and should not be used to judge a specific professional. Labor costs vary with city, seniority, scarcity, taxes, benefits, currency, utilization, and attrition. The [ILO's ILOSTAT](https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/) and [World Bank indicators](https://data.worldbank.org/) provide country context, but neither tells a buyer whether a proposed architect communicates well or a delivery lead can recover a troubled project.

## A better comparison unit

Evaluate the proposed operating system:

1. **work model** — capacity, managed team, or outcome/project;
2. **specialization** — demonstrable depth and relevant references;
3. **collaboration design** — overlap hours, response expectations, decision forums;
4. **commercial maturity** — discovery, estimates, contracts, change control;
5. **delivery evidence** — named leaders, quality controls, security and recovery behavior;
6. **market access** — how the firm earns a credible first conversation.

Region affects constraints and options. It does not predetermine quality. [Chapter 55](55-what-small-firms-can-copy.md) extracts practices that travel across these categories.

## Questions to Think About

1. Which assumptions in the the small global engineering company model would need evidence before a smaller firm acted on it?
2. Which capability should remain accountable inside the engineering firm, even if some work is external?
3. What customer evidence would show that this approach is reducing risk rather than merely adding activity?
