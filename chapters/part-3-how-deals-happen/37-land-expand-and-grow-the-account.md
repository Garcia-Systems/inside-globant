# Chapter 37 — Land, Expand, and Grow the Account

“Land and expand” is used here as a **general industry model**, not a claimed Globant program.

```text
FIRST PROJECT → TRUST → SECOND PROBLEM
→ ADDITIONAL CAPABILITY → LARGER RELATIONSHIP
```

Globant's filing reports large clients at annual-revenue thresholds, customer concentration and long-standing relationships ([FY2024 Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=1557860&owner=exclude)). EA's disclosed longevity and repeated public relationships show that enduring demand exists. [Part II](../part-2-customers/21-from-project-to-strategic-account.md) carefully separates that portfolio evidence from an undocumented expansion sequence.

## Why the first project has option value

A successful engagement can establish supplier onboarding, working relationships, system context, delivery evidence and knowledge of adjacent problems. Globant's breadth across Studios, industries and geographies makes another capability available in principle. Neither breadth nor trust entitles the provider to more work.

Expansion may take several forms: a larger team on the same product, another phase, a new capability such as data or cloud, another geography, or another business unit. Each should be requalified. The next problem may have a different sponsor, competitor, risk and procurement requirement.

Delivery quality is therefore commercial evidence. Engineers who communicate risk, maintain systems and understand the business create confidence no case study can fully substitute. Conversely, missed promises increase the cost of every later sale.

**Reasonable inference:** retained context can lower acquisition and mobilization cost and enable relevant cross-capability introductions. **Unknown:** Globant retention and expansion rates at account level, cross-sold Studios, first-project value, whitespace methods, sales credit and reasons accounts contract.

Growth can become unhealthy if a provider manufactures work, protects legacy choices or creates dependency. Account leadership must connect expansion to customer outcomes, while the portfolio must manage concentration.

## Questions to Think About

1. What evidence from delivery is reusable in a second buying decision?
2. When is cross-selling customer service, and when is it scope seeking?
3. Why should every expansion be requalified?
