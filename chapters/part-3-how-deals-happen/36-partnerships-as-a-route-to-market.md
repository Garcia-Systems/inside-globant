# 36 — Partnerships as a Route to Market

Globant publicly documents relationships with technology-platform companies, but “partner” can mean certification, delivery capability, joint offering, marketing or other arrangements. It does not prove co-selling or generated revenue in every case.

The strongest sources reviewed here establish an expanded Google Cloud collaboration and an AWS partner profile ([Globant–Google Cloud](https://www.globant.com/news/globant-expands-partnership-google-cloud), [AWS profile](https://partners.amazonaws.com/partners/0010h00001h3VppAAE/Globant)). Globant's services and acquisition announcements also document enterprise-platform capability, including Salesforce through CloudShift ([CloudShift](https://www.globant.com/news/globant-acquires-cloudshift)). Part IV will verify current status and investigate additional ecosystems rather than infer a current list from logos.

```text
TECHNOLOGY PLATFORM
        ↓
customer-specific adoption / integration / transformation need

SERVICES PARTNER
        ↓
CUSTOMER OPERATING CHANGE
```

## Why each party might participate

* A platform vendor benefits when customers adopt, consume and succeed with its technology; service firms supply customer-specific design, migration, integration and change.
* Globant gains skills, ecosystem credibility, product knowledge and possible market access while attaching services to platform change.
* A customer can combine platform product capability with an integrator accountable for its particular estate.

These are **general ecosystem incentives**, not proof of a referral or revenue-sharing agreement.

Partnerships can create opportunity through directories, joint events, marketplace visibility, account introductions and joint solution work. They can also constrain design toward one ecosystem and complicate responsibility. Customers should ask who recommends, resells, implements, operates and earns from each component.

**Documented fact:** named relationships and capability investments exist. **Reasonable inference:** some may improve access and technical credibility. **Unknown:** lead volume, co-sell mechanics, fees, certification counts unless currently sourced, marketplace transactions and partner-attributed revenue.

## Questions to Think About

1. How does a customer distinguish recommendation from commercial incentive?
2. Why does platform success create services demand rather than eliminate it?
3. What evidence would prove partner-generated opportunity?
