# 38 — When Sales Becomes Delivery

Signature changes the dominant work, but it should not create an information cliff. The engagement must carry its commercial and technical reasoning into an operating team.

## The handoff record

A robust **general industry model** transfers:

* the business problem, baseline and success measures;
* stakeholders, governance and decision rights;
* scope, deliverables and acceptance;
* architecture choices, rejected options and technical unknowns;
* estimates, staffing logic, dependencies and customer duties;
* commercial terms, milestones and change mechanisms;
* security, privacy, IP and operating constraints;
* relationship commitments not safely left as hallway memory.

Public customer announcements rarely show this boundary. Globant's filing establishes contracts and project-execution risk, while its cases establish selected delivery results; neither publishes a standard handoff process ([FY2024 Form 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/browse/?CIK=1557860&owner=exclude), [customers](https://www.globant.com/customers)).

The central questions are simple: What exactly was promised? Which assumptions supported it? What will success mean? Who owns relationship and delivery decisions? How is change handled when reality differs?

Continuity can be achieved by involving a future delivery leader before commitment, keeping an architect through mobilization, recording decision rationale, or holding a joint customer/provider kickoff. These are **general practices**, not claims about mandatory Globant procedure.

Poor handoff converts sales ambiguity into delivery conflict. The team may optimize output while the sponsor expects outcome; a dependency may appear as new scope; an unavailable specialist may invalidate timing. Transparent revalidation is better than pretending signature froze reality.

> **DELIVERY IS PART OF FUTURE SALES.**

Delivery generates the strongest account evidence: whether the provider behaves well under uncertainty. The account relationship should continue, but not bypass delivery governance or make promises outside controlled change.

## Questions to Think About

1. Which presales participant should remain during mobilization?
2. What promise is hardest to encode in a statement of work?
3. How can change control preserve trust rather than become bureaucracy?
