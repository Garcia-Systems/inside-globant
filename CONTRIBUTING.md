# Contributing Corrections

Corrections that strengthen this research book are welcome, especially for:

- outdated facts or metrics;
- broken or superseded sources;
- misclassified customer, partner, or ecosystem relationships;
- claims that extend beyond their evidence; and
- stronger primary sources.

Every proposed factual correction must include a source link, the source's publication date when available, and a short explanation of what it establishes. Prefer regulatory filings, customer statements, partner directories, and other first-party records over summaries. Please preserve the distinction among documented fact, reasonable inference, general industry model, and unknown.

For editorial fixes, identify the affected file and keep changes focused. Do not add confidential information, speculate about undisclosed relationships, or silently convert current claims into timeless ones.

See [About This Book](ABOUT-THIS-BOOK.md) for the evidence method and the [source register](sources/source-register.md) for claim-level research notes.
